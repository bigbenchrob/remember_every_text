🔧 High-Impact Refinements

1. Remove Redundancy in Bottom Section

Current issue

You are saying the same thing three times:

* Hero: “Nothing… is missing”
* Reconciliation: “No source messages are missing”
* Notes: “No missing messages were detected”

That’s overkill.

⸻

Fix (important)

👉 Delete this line from “Notes & Next Steps”:

“No missing messages were detected.”

Keep only:

MessageLens detected a small overlap while reconciling visible and recovered messages. No source messages are missing.

⸻

Why

* Reduces cognitive noise
* Makes each section feel purposeful
* Avoids “over-defending” correctness (which ironically reduces trust)

⸻

2. Tighten the Reconciliation Card (micro polish)

Current

Result: fully reconciled
No source messages are missing.

A small overlap was detected while reconciling visible and recovered messages. This does not indicate missing data.

Current

⸻

⸻

4. Slight Tone Upgrade in Timeline Section

Current

“If you expected older messages, they may exist…”

⸻

Refined

“If you expected to see older messages, they may exist on another device or in iCloud, but are not present on this Mac.”

* Keeps tone conversational, not technical
