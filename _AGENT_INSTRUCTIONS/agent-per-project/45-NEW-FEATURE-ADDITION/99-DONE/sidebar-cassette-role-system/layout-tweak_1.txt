
#Rules should be:

All cassette content is rendered within the shared content envelope.
Within that envelope, content must declare an alignment mode.

Approved alignment modes:
	•	fill
Content occupies the full width of the content lane.
Used for text blocks and readable content.
	•	leftAnchored
Content aligns to the left rail but does not expand to full width.
Used for labels, links, and lightweight actions.
	•	insetControl
Control elements are aligned to the content lane but rendered with internal horizontal inset so their visual body does not touch the rails.
Used for toggles, segmented controls, dropdowns.
	•	loose
Content aligns to the left rail but may use its intrinsic width.
Used for visualizations such as heatmaps.

Rules:
	•	Content must not define its own external width or padding.
	•	All horizontal alignment must be explainable as a combination of lane policy and alignment mode.
	•	Visual misalignment caused by internal padding must be corrected at the essentials level, not per cassette.
