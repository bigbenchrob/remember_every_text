Reconstruct archive participants and sender handles.

Correct message bucketing is not enough. If the archive rows enter the global timeline without trustworthy participant/sender metadata, the feature will appear to work at the heatmap/search level but become confusing or wrong in actual conversation views. Phase 1 can stay minimal, but it still needs enough participant reconstruction to preserve conversational meaning.

Use the archive chat.db relationships as the authority:

* message.handle_id identifies sender where available
* chat_message_join links messages to chats
* chat_handle_join links chats to participants
* handle.id / handle.service provide participant identifiers
* preserve original archive values even if current Contacts cannot resolve them

Do not attempt fuzzy contact merging in this slice.
